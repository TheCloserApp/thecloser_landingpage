Why TheCloser

Everything you need to close.

No account, no server in the middle. It listens, transcribes, and answers in real time — and stays invisible to everyone else.

Invisible on screen-share

Every window is excluded from capture at the OS level — Zoom, Meet, Teams, and recordings see nothing. No menu-bar icon either.

Answers as they ask

The live transcript streams to the model the moment they finish. Interview mode leads with a verbatim opening line, then tight scannable bullets.

Live transcription

Mic or system audio, transcribed in real time — on-device with Apple Speech, or ElevenLabs Scribe with your key.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can the interviewer see TheCloser when I share my screen?

No. Every TheCloser window, including its menus and popovers, is excluded from screen capture by macOS. Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and screen recorders capture your screen without it, and there's no menu-bar icon either.

Is it really free?

Yes. TheCloser is open source under the MIT license, with no subscription and no account. You add your own API key and pay the AI provider directly for what you use.

Which AI models does it work with?

Anthropic, OpenAI, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, NVIDIA NIM and OpenRouter, all from one model picker. Transcription runs on-device with Apple Speech, or with ElevenLabs Scribe if you add an ElevenLabs key.

Does my data go through your servers?

No. There is no TheCloser server. Your API keys are stored on your Mac, and your transcript goes straight from your Mac to the AI provider you picked.

Does it work with Zoom, Google Meet and Teams?

Yes, and with any other call app. TheCloser listens to your microphone or your Mac's system audio instead of plugging into the call, so it works the same everywhere.

What Mac do I need?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer). The download doesn't run on Intel Macs.

macOS says TheCloser can't be opened. What do I do?

TheCloser isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once. Then allow Microphone, Speech Recognition and Screen Recording when asked.
